# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Researchers, medical physicists, clinicians, collaborators, graduate-program peers, and technical hiring readers who need to quickly understand Udbhav Ram's credibility, research direction, and software practice.

## Product Purpose
The site is a personal portfolio and credibility surface. It should make the first impression memorable, then give readers enough structured proof to trust the clinical AI, medical physics, and software narrative.

## Brand Personality
Precise, kinetic, evidence-backed. The voice should feel technically rigorous and visually distinctive without turning the site into a purely decorative artifact.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic resume pages, muted gray academic portfolios, repeated card grids, gradient-text marketing design, decorative glass effects, and pages that hide the actual work behind vague personality copy.

## Design Principles
- Lead with proof, not ornament.
- Let imagery and structure carry the story before long prose.
- Keep academic credibility legible at scan speed.
- Use contrast and rhythm to make the page memorable.
- Preserve accessibility while increasing visual intensity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, semantic sections, reduced-motion support, responsive reading flow, and no content that depends solely on color or animation.
