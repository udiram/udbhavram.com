# Institutional and issuer marks

Accessed October 8, 2026. Files are retained at their official proportions and colours, shown on a neutral white field, and are never recoloured, stretched, or inverted. The marks identify institutions and issuers connected to the adjacent public record; they do not imply endorsement of this site.

- `uab.svg` — UAB primary logo from the [UAB Brand Guide](https://www.uab.edu/brandguide), downloaded from `https://www.uab.edu/brandguide/images/home/svg/new-primary-logo.svg`.
- `mcmaster.png` — current McMaster University logo from the [McMaster logo guide](https://brand.mcmaster.ca/logos-and-marks/logos-and-marks-cont-mcmaster-logo-minimal-size/), downloaded from `https://brand.mcmaster.ca/app/uploads/2024/03/McMaster-logo.png`.
- `western.svg` — full Western University logo from the official Western site, downloaded from `https://www.uwo.ca/web_standards/2025-global/assets/img/page-titles/svg/full-western-logo.svg`.
- `hamilton-health-sciences.png` — Hamilton Health Sciences site logo, downloaded from `https://www.hamiltonhealthsciences.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/HHS_Logo-e1634831568586.png`.
- `st-josephs-healthcare-hamilton.png` — St. Joseph’s Healthcare Hamilton site logo, downloaded from `https://www.stjoes.ca/GetSiteFile/SJHHLogo.png`.
- `aapm.png` — AAPM core mark served by the official AAPM site, downloaded from `https://www.aapm.org/images/aapm_Q42014.png`.
- `mclaren-racing.svg` — current McLaren Racing header mark served by the official Arrow McLaren site, downloaded from `https://www.mclaren.com/racing/_next/static/media/McLaren-Racing-dark.8f692fa0.svg`.
- `first.svg` — FIRST primary logo from the [FIRST logo library](https://www.firstinspires.org/brand/logos-guidelines), downloaded from `https://info.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/design/first-web/Branding/Primaries/Logo%20Primary.svg`.
- `hosa.webp` — metadata-free web derivative of the standard HOSA–Future Health Professionals logo from the [official HOSA logo page](https://hosa.org/hosa-logos/), downloaded from `https://hosa.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/Cybis-HOSA-Rebrand-Logo-Standard-med-res.jpg`.
- `sps.webp` — metadata-free web derivative of the current Society of Physics Students mark served by the [official SPS site](https://students.aip.org/sps), downloaded through its official AIP image CDN.
- `ssi.webp` — metadata-free web derivative of the SSI corporate mark served by the [official SSI site](https://www.divessi.com/), downloaded from `https://my.divessi.com/assets/corporate/ssi-logo.png`.
- `uab-monogram.svg` — official compact UAB monogram used in narrow record tiles, downloaded from `https://www.uab.edu/brandguide/images/home/svg/new-uab-monogram.svg`.

The UW–Madison mark remains at `public/assets/sourced/uw-madison-logo.png`; its source is the [UW institutional logo guide](https://brand.wisc.edu/resource/uw-institutional-logos-for-web-digital-use/). Organizations without an approved local asset use a typographic initials tile rather than a fabricated logo.
