# Video thumbnail provenance

Checked October 6, 2026. Each JPEG is the public YouTube `hqdefault` preview for the identically named video ID in `src/mediaContent.json`. The copies are served from this site so browsing `/media` does not contact YouTube or `ytimg` before the visitor explicitly loads a video.

The thumbnails are descriptive preview images only. Upload dates, event dates, titles, summaries, and project links remain defined in `src/mediaContent.json`.
