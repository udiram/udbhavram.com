# Brand asset decision inventory

Reviewed October 8, 2026. This is the implementation ledger for institution and issuer treatments used on the site. An official asset is used only when an authoritative organization source supplies it in a suitable form. Missing or restricted artwork is not recreated.

| Organization | Treatment | Decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UW–Madison, McMaster, UAB, Hamilton Health Sciences, Western, St. Joseph’s, AAPM | Official local asset | Official brand guide or organization site; exact asset URLs are recorded in `public/assets/brands/PROVENANCE.md`. |
| McLaren Racing / Arrow McLaren | Official McLaren Racing mark | The current Arrow McLaren site serves the McLaren Racing header mark. The record text retains the exact Arrow McLaren employer name; the site does not fabricate an Arrow-specific lockup. |
| FIRST | Official primary logo | FIRST publishes downloadable logo files and permits FIRST community use subject to its brand guidelines. |
| Society of Physics Students | Official current mark | Served directly by the official AIP/SPS site. |
| HOSA–Future Health Professionals | Official standard logo | HOSA publishes its standard brand and usage guide on its official logo page. |
| SSI | Official corporate mark | Served directly by SSI’s official site. |
| PADI | Named typographic treatment | PADI’s official logo library requires PADI Pro access and its published marketing guidance limits branded-asset use to eligible members. A diver certification alone does not establish a logo licence, so the site uses the name without recreating the mark. |
| College Board | Named typographic treatment | College Board’s trademark guidance states that its logo is not permitted on third-party websites without an express written licence. The award keeps the factual issuer name and no logo. |
| Records with no named issuer | Category treatment | A restrained Honor, Training, Competition, Arts, or Feature tile replaces misleading initials derived from an award title. |
| Other named organizations without a reviewed portable asset | Typographic initials | Initials are clearly rendered as typography, not represented as an official logo. |

All official artwork stays at its supplied aspect ratio and colour. Neutral plates provide clear space and dark-theme stability; no inversion, redrawing, or synthetic mark generation is permitted.
